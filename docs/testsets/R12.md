# R12_marathi_eval_filtered

In-house filtered eval set, 6,282 utterances -- by far the largest test set here (5-6x the next
largest). WER is also far higher here than on any of the other six test sets (50-60%, vs 16-44%
elsewhere), and the ranking flips: `Whisper + Gemma-3-4B` wins clearly (50.44%) despite being
mid-pack or worse everywhere else. See `results/SUMMARY.md` for the full comparison and the
likely explanation (Whisper's much larger pretraining corpus generalizing better to this set's
audio conditions than the Marathi-only fine-tuned encoders do).
